# EP1 — ถ้าคืนลบสิบองศา กล้องความร้อนบอกว่าถนนว่าง อย่าเพิ่งเดินออกไป  (≈ 70 s)

Notes:
- **Fact vs fiction:** fiction (post as "(fiction)"). Facts: normal body temperature ≈ 37 °C; post-mortem cooling
  ≈ 0.8 °C (1.5 °F) per hour in the first hours — the forensic rule of thumb (Glaister equation; real rate depends on
  clothing and air, and slows as the body nears air temperature, so "within days" not a number); thermal imagers
  show temperature *differences*, so an object at ambient temperature blends into the background. −10 °C is a
  normal January night in eastern Ukraine. Check each against a primary source before production.
- **Planted for the twist:** line 9 — two mounds by the well, both visible in the wide yard shot (lines 8–9, 14, 15).
  On the run back (line 15–16) the camera passes the well: still two. Line 21 the reverse wide shows one.
- **Cliffhanger → ep 2 hook:** fresh footprints end at your door → "If footprints stop at your door, don't look through the gap."
